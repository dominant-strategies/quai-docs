## Polaris Fork (October 2026)

The **Polaris Fork** (`go-quai v0.57.0`, activated October 13, 2026 with the updated unlock schedule locking in after October 30, 2026) implemented protocol-level address skips to permanently burn all remaining future genesis allocations belonging to Quai's founders and the Dominant Strategies development team.

### Key Numbers & Structural Impact

- **Tokens Permanently Burned:** **236,456,538 QUAI** forgone by founders and Dominant Strategies.
- **Cumulative Genesis Burn (Singularity + Polaris):** **1,903,616,522 QUAI (~1.90B)** permanently removed from the original 3,000,000,000 genesis allocation—reducing the adjusted genesis baseline to **1,096,383,478 QUAI**.
- **Circulating Supply Share:** Jumped immediately from **~77% → ~93%** of total supply.
- **Annual Genesis Inflation:** Slashed from **~13% → under 4% per year**.
- **Total Annual Inflation (factoring in SOAP):** Reduced from **~18% → under 9% per year**.
- **Monthly Genesis Unlocks:** Collapsed from **~13M → ~3.7M QUAI per month**.
- **Remaining Unvested Overhang:** Cut from **~327M → ~90.4M QUAI (7.2%)**, putting Quai on track for complete genesis vesting by **early 2029** and a Proof-of-Work mining majority by **November 2028**.

## Singularity Fork (March 2026)

Activated at **Prime block 1,530,500** on March 19, 2026 via `go-quai v0.51.0`, the **Singularity Fork** fundamentally restructured both Quai's monetary policy and workshare throughput:

1. **Multilateral Genesis Burn:** Investors, Dominant Strategies, and the Quai Foundation voluntarily agreed to skip and permanently burn **1,667,159,984 QUAI** (81.1% of all remaining future genesis unlocks), moving the vested genesis baseline from **3,000,000,000 QUAI** to **1,332,840,016 QUAI**.
2. **Workshare Inclusion Doubled (16 → 32):** To increase external PoW participation and node capital efficiency under [Project SOAP](/learn/advanced-introduction/soap), `v0.51.0` doubled per-block workshare caps:
   - **Total workshares per block:** `16` → **`32`**
   - **SHA-256 workshares per block:** `8` → **`16`**
   - **Scrypt workshares per block:** `8` → **`16`**
3. **Canonical Supply Formula:** Established the transparent supply framework tracked live at [supply.qu.ai](https://supply.qu.ai):
   $$\text{Total Supply} = \text{Genesis Unlocks} + \text{Mining Emissions} - \text{SOAP Burns}$$

## Intermediate Client Refinements (`v0.52.0` – `v0.55.0`)

Between Singularity and Polaris, iterative `go-quai` releases hardened SOAP economics and reward stability:

- **`v0.52.0`:** Mainnet API stability and post-Singularity RPC enhancements.
- **`v0.53.0`:** Introduced **SHA-equivalent reward normalizations**, preparing SOAP's AuxPoW accounting to accept native Bitcoin proof structures alongside BCH, LTC, DOGE, and RVN.
- **`v0.54.0`:** Implemented a longer **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** to smooth block rewards, difficulty adjustments, and QUAI ↔ Qi conversion dynamics.
- **`v0.55.0`:** Ongoing node synchronization and infrastructure optimizations ahead of Polaris (`v0.57.0`).

## Project SOAP (December 2025)

Launched in December 2025, the **Subsidized Open-market Acquisition Protocol (SOAP)** transformed Quai from a single-algorithm GPU chain into a multi-algorithm Proof-of-Work network secured by KawPoW GPUs alongside SHA-256 and Scrypt ASICs. Rather than letting merge-mined parent-chain rewards generate sell pressure, SOAP routes **100%** of parent-chain coinbase subsidies to protocol-controlled addresses that buy QUAI on the open market and permanently burn it.

Learn more in the [Project SOAP Technical Overview](/learn/advanced-introduction/soap) and track live burns at [soap.qu.ai](https://soap.qu.ai).
